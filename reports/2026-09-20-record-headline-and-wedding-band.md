## Three things in the brief that did not match the files

**The headline the brief called "the record headline" is not in the record
section.** The text it quoted belongs to the statement section, the block under
"Our Unique Standing". The record section has a different headline, about World
Champion titles from Europe, Asia and North America, which was left alone. The
quoted words identified the target beyond doubt, so the work went ahead, but
the naming should be corrected before the next brief refers to it.

**The wedding photograph is not a 3:2 landscape.** The brief said it was, and
said to confirm against the file. It is 574 by 798 pixels, which is upright,
about 5:7. That real ratio was used. Because it stands tall where the other
band photographs are wide, it was given the same fitting treatment the skull
photograph already uses, so the whole picture shows rather than being cropped
to a letterbox. It reads well at both sizes, but it is a different shape from
its neighbours and Scott may want a wider crop instead.

**There is no anchor for the World Bodypainting Festival on the events page**,
so the band links to the About page, as the brief instructed for that case.

Worth knowing separately: the four bands that were already there link to
anchors on the events page that do not exist either. Clicking any of them lands
at the top of that page rather than at the section named. That is not new and
was not touched, but it should probably be fixed.
